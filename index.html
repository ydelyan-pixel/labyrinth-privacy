Privacy Policy

Labyrinth of the Void (com.ofrantic.voidlabyrinth) — last updated: 24 September 2026

Summary

Labyrinth of the Void does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party services in the app. The game runs entirely on your device and works offline.

Information the app collects

None. The app does not ask for, read or send any of the following: name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files, device identifiers, advertising ID, usage or diagnostic data.

Data stored on your device

The game keeps its own progress and settings in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves the device, is not readable by other apps, and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions. It does not require an internet connection to run.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting services and no social network integrations. No data is shared with anyone, because no data is collected.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. It contains no ads and no purchases.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game collects any data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
